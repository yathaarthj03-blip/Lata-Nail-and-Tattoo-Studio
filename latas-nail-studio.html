Our Story

A Sanctuary of Nail Artistry

Since 2019, Lata's Nail Studio & Academy has been Kandivali's destination for premium nail services. Founded with a passion for beauty and a commitment to excellence, we bring together skilled artistry with a warm, hygienic, and welcoming atmosphere.

From flawless gel extensions and intricate nail art to permanent tattoos and professional nail training — every service is delivered with the highest standards. Our studio is not just a beauty destination; it's a place where confidence is crafted.

Our Signature Services

Acrylic Extensions

Long-lasting, perfectly shaped acrylic nail extensions for a stunning, sculpted look that keeps its beauty.

Gel Nail Art

French Manicure

Pedicure

Nail Refill & Repair

Permanent Tattoos

Nail Academy Training
